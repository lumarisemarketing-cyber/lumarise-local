About LumaRise Local

Discover the story behind LumaRise Local.

A place where local businesses can be discovered, supported, and connected with the communities they serve.

Our Purpose

What is LumaRise Local?

Connecting people with businesses in their community.

Our Mission

LumaRise Local was created to make it easier for people to discover local businesses and easier for businesses to get noticed.

From small independent businesses to growing companies, our goal is to create a space where local businesses can showcase what they offer and connect with potential customers.

Why Local Matters

Every local business has a story, a service, or a product that can make a difference in someone's life.

When people choose to support local businesses, they help entrepreneurs grow, create opportunities, and strengthen communities.

Our Values

What We Believe

LumaRise Local is built around three simple ideas.

01

Discover

Make it easier for customers to find businesses, services, and products in their local area.

02

Support

Give local businesses greater visibility and help customers discover new businesses worth supporting.

03

Grow

Create opportunities for businesses to connect with customers and grow their presence in the community.

Find Local. Support Local. Grow Local.

That is more than our tagline. It is the idea behind LumaRise Local and the community we want to build.
